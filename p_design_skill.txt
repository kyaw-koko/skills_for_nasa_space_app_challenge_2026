# Planet X & SPHEREx Presentation Slide Design Skill (`p_design_skill.md`)

## 1. Mission & Domain Context
- **Challenge:** NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 — *Planet X and SPHEREx*
- **Project Identity:** **SPHEREx SkyShift** (Interactive Web-Based Astronomical Discovery & Near-Infrared Sky-Survey Visualization Tool)
- **Core Scientific Pillars:**
  1. **The Planet X Hypothesis:** Gravitational perturbations and orbital clustering (argument of perihelion $\omega$, longitude of ascending node $\Omega$) among extreme Trans-Neptunian Objects (eTNOs) beyond Neptune ($a > 250\text{ AU}$), pointing to a distant Super-Earth / Ice Giant ($5–10\,M_\oplus$).
  2. **NASA SPHEREx Observatory:** Spectro-Photometer for the History of the Universe, Epoch of Reionization and Ices Explorer — an all-sky near-infrared spectral survey ($0.75–5.0\,\mu\text{m}$) capturing **102 spectral bands** via Linear Variable Filters (LVFs) across four all-sky maps over a 2-year nominal mission.
  3. **The Detection Breakthrough:** Moving objects at extreme heliocentric distances exhibit minuscule proper motion ($\sim\text{arcseconds/year}$) and extremely faint optical magnitudes ($V > 22$), but emit thermal/reflected near-infrared signatures detectable via multi-epoch SPHEREx all-sky image differencing, shift-and-stack algorithms, and spectral SED (Spectral Energy Distribution) filtering.

## 2. Visual Aesthetic & Design System (Synthesized from NASA Science & Modern Pitch Templates)

### A. Color Palette (Deep-Space Telemetry & Infrared Contrast)
- **Primary Canvas (Deep Space Void):** `#060913` / `#0B0F19` (High-contrast dark background for astronomical imagery and projection clarity)
- **Elevated Surface / Glass Cards:** `rgba(17, 25, 40, 0.78)` with `1px solid rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.22)` border and `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`
- **Primary Accent (SPHEREx Infrared Cyan):** `#38BDF8` (Orbital tracks, telemetry lines, active HUD indicators)
- **Secondary Accent (Near-IR Thermal Amber / Gold):** `#F59E0B` (Planet X candidate vectors, thermal emission peaks, key metric callouts)
- **Tertiary Accent (Spectral Violet / Hydrogen-Ice):** `#A855F7` (102-band spectroscopy highlights, Kuiper Belt ice signatures)
- **Typography Colors:**
  - Primary Headings: `#F8FAFC` (Crisp Starlight White)
  - Body Copy: `#CBD5E1` (Soft Slate Grey — prevents halation on dark projectors)
  - Muted Captions / Metadata: `#64748B`

### B. Typography & Hierarchy Rules
- **Display / Slide Titles:** Geometric Sans-Serif (`Space Grotesk`, `Inter`, or `Montserrat`), Bold (`700`), tight tracking (`-0.02em`), `36pt–44pt` equivalent on 16:9 canvas.
- **Body & Bullet Copy:** Clean Humanist Sans-Serif (`Inter` or `Plus Jakarta Sans`), Regular/Medium (`400/500`), `18pt–22pt` minimum for projector legibility.
- **Telemetry, Coordinates & Equations:** Monospace (`JetBrains Mono` or `Fira Code`) for RA/Dec coordinates, wavelength bands ($0.75\,\mu\text{m} - 5.0\,\mu\text{m}$), and orbital parameters.
- **Slide Density Rule (The 3x30 Guideline):** Maximum **30–45 words** per content slide; never place walls of text. Every slide must feature **one dominant visual anchor** (orbital diagram, spectral chart, UI mockup, or pipeline flowchart).

### C. Layout Archetypes (Inspired by Editorial Pitch & Scientific Deck Templates)
1. **Hero Title Slide (Full-Bleed Cosmic Canvas):** Edge-to-edge dark planetary/observatory visual on right/background, left-aligned bold title block, mission badge pill (`NASA SPACE APPS 2026 // PLANET X & SPHEREx`), team & project tagline.
2. **Split-Screen Problem/Hook (60/40 Visual-to-Text):** Left 60% shows Kuiper Belt orbital clustering vs. uniform distribution; Right 40% holds 3 crisp problem statements with bold numerical callouts.
3. **Bento Grid Architecture / Data Pipeline:** 3- or 4-card modular grid displaying `IRSA / SPHEREx Archive` $\rightarrow$ `Astropy WCS Alignment` $\rightarrow$ `Shift-and-Stack Motion Filter` $\rightarrow$ `Interactive SkyShift HUD`.
4. **Data & Spectral Comparison Slide:** Clean dark-mode chart comparing Optical Telescopes (narrow FOV, faint $V$-band) vs. SPHEREx (all-sky coverage, 102 IR bands, thermal sensitivity).
5. **Live Demo / Product Showcase Frame:** Browser/HUD frame (`16:9` viewport) showcasing **SPHEREx SkyShift** with floating glassmorphism callout pins pointing to: (a) Time-Epoch Scrubber, (b) 102-Band SED Inspector, (c) Candidate Trajectory Overlay.
6. **Impact & Roadmap Timeline:** Horizontal 3-phase milestone track (`Hackathon MVP` $\rightarrow$ `Citizen Science Mode` $\rightarrow$ `Real-Time SPHEREx Data Release Integration`).

## 4. AI Agent Execution Rules (When Generating Slides or Code)
1. **Strict 16:9 Aspect Ratio:** Design every slide for `1920x1080px` (`16:9`) without vertical scrolling or overflowing text boxes.
2. **Data-First Visuals:** Never use generic clip-art or decorative filler shapes. Every graphic must encode orbital mechanics, infrared wavelengths, telescope FOV geometry, or actual UI telemetry.
3. **Bilingual & Technical Precision:** Keep astronomical terms (`Right Ascension`, `Declination`, `Perihelion`, `Linear Variable Filter`, `102 Spectral Bands`, `IRSA`) accurate; when presenting in Burmese, pair clean Myanmar explanations with standard international astrophysics notation.
4. **High-Contrast Projector Safety:** Ensure text contrast ratio $\ge 7:1$ against dark space backgrounds; avoid thin light-grey fonts on dark blue gradients.
5. **Canva / HTML Translation Ready:** Structure every slide output with explicit **Layout Grid**, **Headline**, **Max 3 Bullet Points**, **Visual Asset Prompt/Chart Spec**, and **Speaker Notes** so it can be built immediately in HTML/Reveal.js or Canva.
